# Resona 0.1.0 — first release

Resona records what the Spotify desktop app plays on Windows, one tagged file per track, and tells you which tracks came out **bit-perfect**. It is a Rust rewrite of [Spytify](https://github.com/jwallet/spy-spotify), with a new interface and a lossless pipeline.

## Highlights

- 🎙️ **Spotify's audio only.** Per-process capture: notifications, videos and other apps never end up in your files.
- 🎯 **Lossless, checked.** FLAC up to 24-bit / 44.1 kHz with Spotify Premium in Lossless. Every sample is analysed, and each track gets a badge: *bit-perfect 16/24-bit*, *peaks limited by Spotify*, *slightly changed by Spotify* or *altered*.
- 📋 **Playlist mode.** Paste a playlist (private ones too), album or track link: Resona starts it in Spotify, records every track, pauses Spotify at the end and offers to retry what was missed.
- 🏷️ **Exact tags.** Album, album artists, track number, date, copyright and a 640 px cover of the release actually played, from Spotify's catalogue; genre, ISRC and label from Deezer.
- 🎤 **Lyrics.** Synced lyrics from LRCLIB, saved as a `.lrc` next to each track (on by default).
- 🔌 **Virtual cable mode** (optional, VB-Cable): Spotify plays silently into the cable, set up by Resona, so your headset's settings no longer touch the recording and you can listen to something else.
- 🛡️ **Checks before recording.** Spotify's volume (one click sets it to 100 %), normalization, Automix, quality, Windows audio enhancements and the output device's sample rate.
- FLAC, WAV or MP3 · folders by artist / album · track or order numbers · tracks already recorded skipped · ads never recorded · English and French · runs from the notification area · update check.

## Install

Download **`resona_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe`** below and run it. It installs for your user, without administrator rights.

The installer and `resona.exe` are signed, with a self-signed certificate that Windows does not recognise as a publisher yet: SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"* — click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksum of the installer.

## Requirements

- **Windows 11** (the per-process capture needs build 20348 or later).
- **The Spotify desktop app**, logged in. Playlist mode, exact tags and the volume check use `spotify_cli`, which the Microsoft Store version of Spotify ships.

For lossless recordings: Spotify in **Lossless**, volume at maximum, normalization, equalizer, crossfade and Automix off; in Windows, audio enhancements off and the output device at 44,100 Hz — or the virtual cable mode.

## Known limitations

- Recording happens in real time: a one-hour playlist takes an hour.
- Not tested yet: Windows 10, a Spotify Free account (ad handling), podcasts, and the classic installer of Spotify from spotify.com (whether it ships `spotify_cli`).
- Changing the output device in the middle of a recording is not handled.
