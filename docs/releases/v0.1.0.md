# Resona 0.1.0 — first release

Resona records what the Spotify desktop app plays on Windows, one tagged file per track, and tells you which recordings came out **bit-perfect**.

It's a Rust rewrite of [Spytify](https://github.com/jwallet/spy-spotify), with a new interface, playlist automation and a lossless-focused recording pipeline.

## Highlights

- 🎙️ **Spotify's audio only.** Per-process capture keeps notifications, videos and every other app out of your recordings.
- 🎯 **Lossless, verified.** Record FLAC up to 24-bit / 44.1 kHz with Spotify Premium in Lossless. Every track is analysed and labelled *bit-perfect 16/24-bit*, *peaks limited by Spotify*, *slightly changed by Spotify* or *altered*.
- 📋 **Paste a playlist and walk away.** Resona starts a playlist, album or track in Spotify, records each song separately, pauses when it's finished and retries anything that was missed. Private playlists work too.
- 🏷️ **Release-accurate metadata.** Album, album artists, track number, release date, copyright and the 640 px cover of the release actually played come from Spotify; genre, ISRC and label are completed with Deezer.
- 🎤 **Synced lyrics.** LRCLIB lyrics are saved automatically as `.lrc` files next to your tracks.
- 🔌 **Optional virtual cable mode.** Route Spotify silently through VB-Cable so your headset settings cannot affect the recording while you listen to something else.
- 🛡️ **Signal checks before recording.** Resona checks Spotify's volume, normalization, Automix, audio quality, Windows enhancements and your output sample rate before you start.

Also included: FLAC, WAV and MP3 · artist / album folders · track or recording-order numbering · duplicate handling · English and French UI · background recording from the notification area · update checking.

## Install

Download **`resona_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe`** below and run it. Resona installs for your user only — no administrator rights required.

> [!NOTE]
> The installer and `resona.exe` are signed with a self-signed certificate. Windows does not recognise it as a trusted publisher yet, so SmartScreen may show *"Windows protected your PC"* on first launch. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the installer checksum.

## Requirements

- **Windows 11** — per-process capture requires build 20348 or later.
- **Spotify desktop**, logged in.

Playlist mode, Spotify metadata and the volume check use `spotify_cli`, included with the Microsoft Store version of Spotify. Recording itself still works without it.

## For lossless recordings

Use Spotify **Lossless** with:

- volume at 100 %
- normalization off
- equalizer off
- crossfade off
- Automix off
- Windows audio enhancements off
- output device set to 44,100 Hz

Or enable **virtual cable mode**, which isolates the recording from your normal output device.

## Known limitations

- Recording happens in real time — a one-hour playlist takes one hour.
- Spotify Free (ads), podcasts, Windows 10 and the classic Spotify installer from spotify.com have not been fully tested yet.
- Changing the Windows output device during an active recording is not currently handled.
